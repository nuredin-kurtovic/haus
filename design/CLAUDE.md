# Project instructions

## Copy
- **Never use em dashes (—) anywhere.** Not in body copy, headings, table cells, or bullet glyphs. Use periods, commas, colons, or restructure the sentence. En dashes in numeric/time ranges (08:00–18:00, 6–12) are fine.
- Language is Bosnian. Voice follows the HAUS brandbook: short sentences, numbers instead of adjectives, "Vi" in every direct address, no corporate or trade jargon.

## HAUS project facts (decided by the user, override the source PDFs)
- Packages are **HAUS Mini** (59 KM), **HAUS Plus** (169 KM), **HAUS Pro** (390 KM po stanu). No "Osnovni / Plus / Iznajmljivač" labels.
- **No karenca.** The 15-day waiting period is removed from the model entirely.
- **No phone numbers and no calling.** Everything goes through the HAUS mobile app: fault reports, termini, notifications, dispatcher contact. The brandbook master promise is reworded to "Jedna prijava. Poznata cijena. Dogovoren rok. Pisana garancija."
- Payment: uplatnica na mejl or kartica only. No cash.
- Multi-city, not Sarajevo only. Cities are managed in the admin panel and shown on a public map.

## Visual
- Brandbook rules are binding: Poppins only, no rounded corners anywhere, ember (#FE5100) is a plane and never a thin line, ember and ink (#252422) never touch (white or ivory #FFFCF2 field between them), no light text on sand (#CCC5B9), small text never sits directly on ember.
- Logo files: `logo-primary.svg` (ember symbol, ink wordmark, for light backgrounds) and `logo-ivory.svg` (all ivory, for ink backgrounds).
